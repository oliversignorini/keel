---
title: "Why organisations, not just users"
description: "Multi-tenancy is easy to bolt on late and expensive to skip early. Here's why Keel builds it in from Phase 3."
date: "2026-01-12"
author: "Keel team"
---

Most SaaS products eventually need to answer "who is this data for — a
person, or a team?" Retrofitting an organisation model onto a
single-tenant schema means a migration that touches every table with a
foreign key to `User`.

Keel starts with organisations as the tenant boundary. Every resource
belongs to an organisation, membership carries a role, and roles map to
permissions checked on the server — never just hidden in the UI.

The switcher in the top bar keeps the current organisation visible at all
times, because "wrong data" confusion in a multi-tenant app is almost
always a context problem, not a permissions bug.

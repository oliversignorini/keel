---
title: "Stripe is the source of truth"
description: "Keel's billing layer treats local plan and subscription rows as a cache, not a database — here's what that buys you."
date: "2026-02-03"
author: "Keel team"
---

It's tempting to model plans and prices as rows you own, and sync Stripe
into them once. It works fine until a price changes in the Stripe
dashboard and your app keeps quoting the old one.

Keel's `sync_stripe_plans` job refreshes local plan rows nightly, and the
pricing page reads from them directly — but every number displayed is one
that Stripe could recompute independently. Checkout, the customer portal,
and the webhook handlers all defer to Stripe for anything that touches
money.

The result: a local cache that can be wrong for at most a day, and never
a local ledger you have to reconcile by hand.
